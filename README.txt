This app brings Salus Quantum thermostats into Homey so you can see room temperature and humidity together with the rest of your home automation. Use the same Salus account you already use on your phone, add your thermostats during pairing, and readings refresh about once a minute.

Besides monitoring climate readings, you can set the target temperature and switch the thermostat on or off from Homey. If you need help, use the support contact or issue link shown on the app page in the Homey App Store.
