This app brings your Salus Smart Home devices into Homey: Quantum thermostats, smart plugs and underfloor heating wiring centres. Use the same Salus account you already use on your phone, add your devices during pairing, and readings refresh about once a minute.

Control your thermostats' target temperature, switch them on or off, and choose between following their schedule or a permanent hold. See per-room heating and cooling activity, battery levels, and build flows on them. Smart plugs can be switched and report their energy use, and wiring centres show pump, boiler and zone status.

If you need help, use the support contact or issue link shown on the app page in the Homey App Store.
